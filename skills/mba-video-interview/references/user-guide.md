# User instructions

Write for the applicant. School-specific names, resources, and timing.
Keep the section order below so every school feels like the same product.

## Required sections

1. **What this is** — one-way video, not a conversation
2. **Format** — question mix + think/answer times (practice vs official)
3. **How to excel by prompt type** — frameworks with second budgets
4. **Success strategies** — authenticity, fresh stories, timing, camera
5. **Mistakes**
6. **Story bank to build before start**
7. **Sample strong responses** (2–3, school-specific, ~60s spoken)
8. **Final reminders**

## Answer frameworks (adapt times to `{ANSWER}`)

**Intro / personality**

- Lead with something **not** on the resume
- Do not recap title / company / years

**Why this program** (only if that prompt appears)

- 2–3 named resources (professor, lab, club, course, trek)
- Each tied to a concrete goal
- Alumni/student conversation if they have one
- No rankings-only answers

**Behavioral — compact STAR** (for a 60s cap)

- Situation 10s → Action 35s → Result & learning 15s
- “I” not only “we”
- One number, name, or timeframe

**Community / values**

- Concrete example first, value claim second
- Show collaboration / impact, don’t announce it

## Story bank (always)

- 30s “who I am beyond the resume”
- 2–3 school resources tied to goals
- 4–5 fresh STAR stories not in written essays: leadership, conflict,
  failure, risk, community
- Why MBA **now**

## Camera (always)

- Look at the lens, not the self-view
- Face the light
- Conversational pace, slightly slower than feels natural
- Stumble + recover is fine; note-reading is not

## Tone

AdCom last look: authenticity over polish. Do not promise that official
prompts are the ones in this bank if the school keeps them unpublished.
