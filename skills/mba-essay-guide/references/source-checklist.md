# Source checklist

Work top to bottom. For each family, run the suggested searches, fetch full pages, and pull the listed items into the reconciliation table. Replace `[School]` with the common short name (Kellogg, Booth, Wharton, HBS, Sloan, Ross, Fuqua, Tuck, Haas, Stern, CBS, Darden, Yale SOM, Anderson, INSEAD, LBS, and so on) and `[School full]` with the formal name when a query needs it.

Search tips that hold across schools:
- Include the cycle, for example `2026-2027`, in at least one query per family. Consultants title their posts by cycle.
- Run one query without the cycle to surface the school's older design posts.
- Prefer `web_search` (thorough) over the fast variant for this task; the question lists live deep in pages, not in snippets.
- Fetch the page whenever a snippet mentions a list, a table, or a quote from an admissions officer.

---

## Tier 1: The school itself [Official]

**What to pull**
- Exact current prompts and word limits, verbatim
- Video essay mechanics: number of questions, prep seconds, answer seconds, total time, when it unlocks, deadline offset, retake policy, practice mode
- Any quoted framing ("we want to see the person behind...") and stated criteria ("no perfect answers", "holistic")
- Older blog posts that explain why the component exists, who reviews it, and the order of review
- Any published sample or fixed questions, even from prior cycles
- AI-use policy text

**Where**
1. Admissions "How to Apply" or "Application Process" page for the full-time MBA
2. The school's admissions blog or news section. Common paths: `/news/blog/`, `/blog/admissions/`, `/insider/`, `/admissions-blog/`
3. The application portal instructions, often quoted verbatim by Clear Admit and mbaMission if you cannot log in
4. The school's YouTube channel (application walkthroughs, "ask admissions" sessions)
5. Archived FAQ text on consultant sites (Aringo keeps old official FAQs)

**Searches**
- `[School] MBA how to apply essays`
- `[School] admissions blog video essay`
- `[School] admissions blog essay tips`
- `[School] application video essay 2019` (and 2021, 2023) to surface design-era posts
- `site:[school domain] video essay` if the engine honors it; otherwise `[school domain] video essay blog`

---

## Tier 2: Admissions officers on record [Adcom]

**What to pull**
- Named person, title, date, venue
- Why the component exists, what they say they look for, what they say they do not expect
- Any stated review order or weighting

**Where**
1. Menlo Coaching's school profile pages (they interview deans and directors on video)
2. Poets&Quants interviews and "meet the adcom" pieces
3. Clear Admit "Admissions Director Q&A" series
4. MBA Insight Q&A series
5. mbaMission and Stacy Blackman conference recaps (AIGAC)
6. School podcasts and LinkedIn posts by the admissions director

**Searches**
- `[School] admissions director interview video essay`
- `[School] assistant dean admissions interview essays`
- `Poets Quants [School] admissions director`
- `Clear Admit admissions director Q&A [School]`
- `MBA Insight [School] admissions`

---

## Tier 3: Applicant-reported questions [Reported]

**What to pull**
- Question lists as compiled by applicants, with the cycle they refer to and the compiler
- Any reported timing details that differ from official text (prep seconds often change quietly)
- Debriefs that describe what the experience felt like

**Where**
1. GMAT Club school forum, especially pinned "video essay questions" threads and the cross-school "collection of all MBA video essay questions" thread
2. GMAT Club interview debriefs for the school
3. Reddit r/MBA (search the school name plus "video essay" or "essay")
4. Clear Admit LiveWire and ApplyWire
5. Consultant posts that explicitly say "our clients were asked" (mark these as Reported, not Consultant)

**Searches**
- `GMAT Club [School] video essay questions [year]`
- `reddit MBA [School] video essay questions`
- `[School] video essay debrief`
- `Clear Admit LiveWire [School] video essay`

**Handling**
- Keep the list intact under its own heading with compiler and date
- Note whether the list predates a format change
- If the user is asking within two weeks of a round deadline, say that fresh reports are likely imminent

## Tier 4: Consulting firms [Consultant]

Cover at least six. The first four are near-mandatory because they update every cycle and are widely read.

| Firm | URL pattern | Strength | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aringo | aringo.com/[school]-video-essay-interview/, aringo.com/[school]-business-school-mba/ | Evaluation criteria lists, archived official FAQs | Some pages keep old timing figures |
| Stacy Blackman (SBC) | stacyblackman.com/blog/[school]-mba-essay-tips/ | Former adcom on staff, strong read on why formats change | Essay-focused; video coverage is brief |
| mbaMission | mbamission.com/blog/how-to-get-into-[school]-essay-tips-and-examples/ | Quotes portal text verbatim, conference Q&A with adcom | Long; skim to the video section |
| Vantage Point MBA | vantagepointmba.com/application-advice/[school]-video-essay-questions/ | Longest practice question lists, updated close to deadlines | Practice list, not a reported list |
| Leland | joinleland.com/library/a/[school]-mba-video-essay | Question categories, answer templates, delivery tips | Generic in places |
| Menlo Coaching | menlocoaching.com/top-mba-programs/[school]/ | Adcom interviews, admissions basics | Interview dates can be old; check |
| Clear Admit | clearadmit.com/mba-admissions-essay-topics-analyses/[school]-mba-essay-topic-analysis/ | Verbatim portal text, word-limit quirks | Sometimes not updated for format changes |
| Accepted | blog.accepted.com/[school]-mba-essay-tips-deadlines/ | Deadlines, class profile, points to school videos | Light on video |
| Personal MBA Coach | personalmbacoach.com/[school]-mba-application-essay-analysis/ | Former adcom on staff, clear "what changed" summaries | Brief |
| Sam Weeks Consulting | samweeks.com/post/[school]-mba-essay-questions-analysis | Client-reported themes, SCAR framework | Mixed cycles on one page |
| Stratus Admissions | stratusadmissionscounseling.com/blog/tips-for-your-[school]-application-essays/ | AIGAC conference context | Brief |
| Ellin Lolis | ellinlolis.com/blog/ | Long-form video essay guides | Older quotes from past formats |
| Fortuna Admissions | fortunaadmissions.com | Former adcom essays | Coverage varies by school |
| Admissionado, ApplicantLab, Gatehouse | various | Occasional deep dives | Check dates |

**Searches**
- `[School] MBA essay tips [cycle]`
- `[School] video essay questions tips`
- `[Firm name] [School] essay analysis`

**What to pull from each**
- Their read on what changed and why
- Evaluation criteria they name
- Practice question lists (deduplicate against Tier 3)
- Prep methods with the firm named
- Post date and last-updated date

## Tier 5: Platform (optional)

If the school uses a video platform (Kira Talent is common), the vendor's documentation explains mechanics like question randomization and retake logic. Useful for practice-tool builders; one line in the format table is enough for applicants.
